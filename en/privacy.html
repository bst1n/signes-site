Privacy

IN ONE SENTENCE

Signes collects nothing. No account, no tracker, no usage analytics, no server. Your ideas never leave your device.

WHAT IS STORED, AND WHERE

Your notes, tags and settings are saved locally on your iPhone.

If you turn on iCloud sync, they are also stored in your own iCloud storage, under your Apple account. The publisher has no access to it.

WHAT IS NOT COLLECTED

No personal data, no advertising identifier, no usage analytics, no automatic reports. The app embeds no third-party tracking kit.

NETWORK CONNECTIONS

Signes works offline. The app contacts no server belonging to the publisher.

SHARING AND EXPORT

Images are produced on your device. They are only handed to the app you pick yourself in the iOS share sheet. Likewise, a Markdown export goes only where you choose to send it.

PURCHASES

Purchases are handled by Apple. The publisher receives neither your payment method nor your identity.

YOUR RIGHTS

Since no personal data is collected by the publisher, there is nothing to access, correct or delete with them. Your content stays under your control: you can export or erase it from the app and from your iCloud settings.
